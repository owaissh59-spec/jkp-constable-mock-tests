# Syllabus — Written Test for the post of Constable in J&K Police (Executive Police / Armed / IRP / SDRF)

> Source: Official JKSSB / J&K Police notification, **Annexure-A** — Syllabus for Written Test for the post of Constables (Executive Police/Armed/IRP/SDRF), dated 30/09/2024.
> This is the authoritative reference for all mock-test generation. Do not alter the exam blueprint.

## Exam Blueprint

- The written test comprises **100 objective-type, multiple-choice questions**.
- **Total marks: 100** — i.e. **1 mark per question**.
- **Duration: 02:00 hours (120 minutes).**
- **Negative marking:** none is prescribed in the official syllabus/notification. Practise on the assumption that there is no penalty for a wrong answer, so **no question should be left unattempted** — but never guess blindly where elimination is possible.

| S.No. | Section | Subject | No. of Questions | Marks |
|-------|---------|---------|------------------|-------|
| 1 | A | General English | 25 | 25 |
| 2 | B | General Knowledge and Current Affairs (India) | 25 | 25 |
| 3 | C | General Knowledge with special reference to J&K | 10 | 10 |
| 4 | D | Numerical and Reasoning Ability | 25 | 25 |
| 5 | E | Basic Concepts of Computers | 15 | 15 |
| | | **Total** | **100** | **100** |

**Level note:** The Constable post carries an essential qualification of **10+2 (Higher Secondary)**. Accordingly, **all five sections are set at 10+2 / matriculation-plus level** — the questions test fundamental concepts and application, not graduation-level depth. This is a key difference from the Sub-Inspector paper, where Parts A, B and D are pitched at graduation level.

---

## A. General English (25 Questions, 25 Marks) — 10+2 level

The official syllabus lists the following areas:

- Articles
- Clauses
- Pronouns
- Homonyms / homophones
- Tenses
- Punctuation
- Synonyms and antonyms
- Analogies
- Idioms and Phrases
- Uses of prepositions

> Note: "Clauses" is listed twice in the official document. It is treated here as a single area covering clause types (main/subordinate, noun/adjective/adverb clauses) and their correct use.

## B. General Knowledge and Current Affairs (India) (25 Questions, 25 Marks) — 10+2 level

- Important dates in Indian History / Freedom Struggle, different dates and events
- First in world (Adventure, Sports, Discoveries)
- First in India (Adventure, Sports, Discoveries)
- Popular names of Personalities (Religion, Politics, Scientific discoveries, Geographical, Sports, History)
- The Newspaper world — current Dailies & Weeklies of India
- Books & Authors — General
- Famous Places in India
- Languages
- Capitals & Currencies
- United Nations Organizations — Veto Powers, number of countries as its members, principal organs and their functions
- SAARC, ASEAN
- Everyday Science
- World famous Awards — (1) in Science, (2) in Literature, (3) in Sports
- National Awards — (1) in Science, (2) in Literature, (3) in Sports
- The world of Sports
- Climate & Crops in India
- Constitution of India (Formation, Fundamental Rights, Directive Principles)
- Democratic institutions
- Forms of Government
- Political & Physical divisions of world & India
- Important rivers & lakes in India
- Current Events of National and International Level
- Agriculture in economic development; Industrialization and economic development
- Centrally Sponsored Schemes — Guidelines and Objectives
- Indian Foreign Trade

## C. General Knowledge with special reference to J&K (10 Questions, 10 Marks) — 10+2 level

- Abbreviations, Important dates, popular names of personalities and their achievements/contribution (National and International)
- Weather, Climate, Crops, Means of Transport; Important power projects and their impact on the J&K economy
- Rivers and Lakes
- Important Tourist Destinations
- History of J&K — historical places of J&K and their importance
- RTI Act
- Indus Water Treaty and its impact on the economy
- Agriculture in economic development, industrialization and economic development
- Current events of Local, National and International importance

## D. Numerical and Reasoning Ability (25 Questions, 25 Marks) — 10+2 level

> Candidates will be tested primarily on **fundamental mathematical concepts and application-oriented reasoning**. The broad areas are:

### Basic Arithmetic
- Number System
- Percentage
- Average
- Profit & Loss
- Ratio & Proportion
- Speed, Distance and Time
- Mathematical reasoning
- Basic Algebra
- Mensuration
- Decimal Fractions
- Simple and Compound Interest
- Trigonometry
- Simplification

### Reasoning Ability
- Number series
- Letter series
- Coding decoding
- Direction sense
- Blood relations
- Statements and conclusions
- Logical Reasoning
- Mental Reasoning
- Sequential output tracing
- Assertions and reasons
- Arithmetical Operations

## E. Basic Concepts of Computers (15 Questions, 15 Marks) — 10+2 level

> The candidates' understanding of the concepts of computers and their application will be tested, broadly covering:

- Computer terminology
- Hardware and Software
- Input and Output Devices
- MS Word and MS Excel
- Storage and Operating systems
- Safety and Security
- E-mail and Internet Usage
- Search Engines

## Differences from the Sub-Inspector paper (for reference)

| | Constable | Sub-Inspector |
|---|---|---|
| Questions | 100 | 100 |
| Marks per question | 1 | 2 |
| Total marks | 100 | 200 |
| Negative marking | Not prescribed | 0.5 per wrong answer |
| Duration | 120 min | 120 min |
| Sections | 5 (A–E) | 6 (A–F) |
| Blueprint | 25 : 25 : 10 : 25 : 15 | 20 : 20 : 15 : 15 : 15 : 15 |
| Level | 10+2 throughout | Graduation (A, B, D) + 10th (C, E, F) |
| Maths & Reasoning | Combined in one 25Q section (D) | Split across A (Reasoning), C (Quant), E (Maths) |
| English | Grammar-driven (articles, clauses, pronouns, tenses, punctuation, prepositions) — **no comprehension passage prescribed** | Comprehension-driven (passages, cloze, para jumbles, narration) |
| J&K GK | Separate dedicated 10Q section (C) | Folded into General Awareness (B) |
