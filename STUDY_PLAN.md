>

>
> **Blueprint:** A General English 25 · B GK & Current Affairs (India) 25 · C GK — J&K 10 · D Numerical and Reasoning Ability 25 · E Basic Concepts of Computers 15.
>
> **How to use each day:** In the morning, revise the topic(s)/section(s) listed for that day. Then attempt the day's mock tests in order (Morning → Afternoon → Late). To generate a test, ask: *"Generate the next mock test."* The system picks the next ⬜ test below automatically.
>
> **Total planned tests:** 207  ·  ⬜ = pending  ·  ✅ = generated

**Difficulty profiles:** `foundation` (easy-tilted) · `standard` (blueprint) · `advanced` (hard-tilted) · `exam` (full-length simulation). All sections are pitched at **10+2 level** — difficulty means more steps and subtler distractors, never higher theory.

## Key Tips for Success

1. Focus heavily on **D — Numerical & Reasoning (25 marks)** and **B — GK India (25 marks)** as they carry the highest weightage.
2. For **GK J&K (10 marks):** Focus on rivers, lakes, history, RTI Act, and Indus Water Treaty — these are frequently asked.
3. **Computer section (15 marks)** is scoring — MS Word, MS Excel shortcuts, and internet terms are easy to memorize.
4. Practice at least one full-length mock every week from Day 39 onwards to build exam stamina.
5. Maintain a separate **'Error Log'** — note every question you get wrong and revise it weekly.
6. For Current Affairs: Read daily newspapers (Greater Kashmir, Rising Kashmir) and follow J&K-specific updates.
7. Time management in exam: Spend ~30 sec per question. Do easy ones first, mark difficult ones for review.
8. Since no negative marking is prescribed — make sure **no question is left unattempted**.
9. Sleep 7–8 hours daily. Burnout will hurt more than extra hours of cramming.
10. On exam day: Carry all documents, reach early, read each question twice before answering.
